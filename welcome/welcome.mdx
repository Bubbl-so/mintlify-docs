## What is Bubbl?
Bubbl is the OS powering the world’s most sought-after in-person experiences.

At it's core, it's a social-first ticketing and event platform built to empower real-life connections through curated, high-quality experiences. Bubbl focuses on exclusivity, and community building, helping organizers grow their audience, monetize effectively, and produce unforgettable events.
